What does Orbizion do?

Orbizion is a digital agency in Pakistan that builds fast websites and mobile apps, and provides SEO, Meta Ads and branding for clinics, schools and growing businesses. Cybersecurity and AI work is available on special request only.

How much does SEO cost in Pakistan?

SEO cost depends on your website, competition and goals. Orbizion starts with a free audit, then gives a fixed monthly plan based on the keywords, pages and local areas you want to rank for.

How long does SEO take to work?

Most websites see early improvements in 4 to 8 weeks, and steady growth in 3 to 6 months. Technical fixes and local SEO usually show results first, while competitive keywords take longer.

Do you work with clinics and schools?

Yes. Orbizion works with clinics, schools and growing businesses in Pakistan and abroad, with local SEO, Google Business Profile setup, and pages that turn searches into calls, visits and enquiries.

Can you build a website and do SEO for it?

Yes. Orbizion builds the website first, then makes it findable with technical SEO, local SEO, content and speed work, so you do not need a separate developer and a separate SEO agency.
