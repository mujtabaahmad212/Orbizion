Frequently asked questions

How do parents find my school on Google?

Parents search for admissions, a class and a place, for example school admissions in Peshawar. Local SEO and clear admissions pages help your school appear in those results.

What should a school website include?

Classes, fees, admissions dates, documents, transport, facilities and contact details. Every page should answer one parent question and have one clear next step.
